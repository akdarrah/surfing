# Lessons

## 2026-10-09 — "wow me" means presentation and level design first
- v1/v1.1 shipped the cheapest version of every feature (flat low-poly look, six straight identical ramps, guns bolted on). Adam rejected it: not modern, level dull, shooting a third wheel.
- Rule: when the brief says "modern", "wow", "all out", or "viral", budget the first and biggest effort for the look and the level, not the netcode. Research the genre's best examples before designing a level, and never ship a level that is a straight line of one primitive.
- Rule: every feature must serve the core loop. If a mechanic (guns) doesn't make the race better, cut it rather than tune it.
- Rule: sound and full screen are part of "feels like a game", not polish to defer.
